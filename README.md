## 🎮 About the Game

An exciting fishing simulator for your phone! Explore **30 unique islands**, each with its own fish. Catch everything from common minnows to mythical Leviathans and Golden Fish.

## ✨ Features

- 🏝️ **30 islands** — from Quiet Bay to the Island of Gods
- 🎣 **32 rods** — from a stick to the TITAN rod
- 🪱 **30 baits** — buy 1 to 500 at a time
- 🐟 **175+ fish species** + 30 bosses
- 🏆 **19 achievements** and 20 quests
- 🎈 **12 bobbers** — from red to divine
- 🎮 **Simple controls** — just tap the screen
- 📱 **Works offline** — no internet needed
- 💾 **Auto-save** — progress is never lost
- 👥 **3 profiles** — with export/import

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Cast your rod** — wait for a bite
2. **Hook it** — tap "HOOK!" when you see "PUSH IT, FASTER!"
3. **Hold the tension** — keep the bar in the green zone
4. **Sell your fish** — earn coins
5. **Upgrade** — buy new gear and unlock islands
